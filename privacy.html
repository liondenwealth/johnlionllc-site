Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

This policy explains how John Lion LLC ("we", "us") handles information in our iPhone apps: Sono Bestie, Out The Chat and Back 2 The Past. Our approach is simple: keep your information on your device whenever we can, collect as little as possible, and never sell it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or show third-party ads.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Sono Bestie

Sono Bestie doesn't require an account. Your notes, progress and settings are stored on your device. Some optional features, such as local weather and job search, may use your approximate location or your search terms to work. These are shared with outside service providers only when you use those features, and they handle that information under their own privacy policies. You can turn off location access any time in your iPhone's Settings. Subscriptions, if you buy one, are processed by Apple; we don't receive your payment details. Sono Bestie is a reference tool for sonography professionals and students. It isn't a medical device, so please don't enter patient information.

Out The Chat

Out The Chat is currently in testing. To use it, you sign in with your phone number and create a profile (first name and an animal). Your location is shared only while sharing is on, and only with verified members of the crews you share it with. You can pause sharing at any time. If you add medical information and choose to share it, your crew can see it. Alerts you send are stored with your crew.

  • Location trails and last-seen positions are kept up to 24 hours (a crew can set a shorter time). Chat messages are kept 30 days. Event crews are deleted when they end.
  • Alert records are kept for safety purposes. If you delete your data, they are kept without a link to your account.
  • Crowd-level counts for venue staff are off unless you turn them on in Settings.
  • "Delete my data" in Settings removes your account and what you wrote. Notifications never include exact locations or medical details.
  • Maps use Apple Maps. To draw the map and search for places, Apple Maps receives the area on screen and the words you type in search. Your crew's positions are never sent to it.
  • Kids under 13 need a parent in their crew.

Out The Chat supplements emergency services and venue security. It never replaces them. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Back 2 The Past

With your permission, Back 2 The Past reads your Photos library to help you sort and post photos and videos. Sorting and image analysis happen on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are not uploaded to us. If you turn on calendar sync or reminders, the app adds events to your calendar and schedules notifications on your device. Your categories, captions and settings are stored on your device.

Permissions

Our apps ask before using your Photos, camera, location, calendar or notifications, and only use them for the feature you're using. You can change these permissions any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can delete on-device data by deleting the app. To have account data deleted (for example in Out The Chat), use "Delete my data" in the app or email us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above.
