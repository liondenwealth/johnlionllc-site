Back 2 The Past

Your camera roll, ten minutes at a time.

Back 2 The Past sorts your photos and videos in short sessions, helps you pick the best ones and gets them ready to post, so the backlog stops being a wall.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

Three steps, one decision at a time.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for people who shoot a lot and post a little.

⏱️

Made for the time you have

Pick 5, 10, 20 or 30 minutes. You get a session sized to fit, and it saves the moment you stop.

👆

One swipe at a time

Right to keep, left to trash, up to post. You never stare at the whole pile.

🎬

Videos count too

Videos are sorted, played and shared at full quality, right alongside your photos.

🔒

Private by design

Sorting happens on your iPhone with Apple's built-in image recognition. Nothing is uploaded.

🧹

Clears the clutter

Finds near-duplicates, blurry shots and old screenshots, and helps you keep the best one.

📮

Ready to post

Crop previews for every platform, saved hashtags, a grid preview and a post queue.

📅

Plan your week

Schedule posts, get a reminder and sync with the calendar you already use.

📷

Looks with a mood

A camera with 90s-inspired film, R&B and camcorder looks, baked into your photos and videos.
